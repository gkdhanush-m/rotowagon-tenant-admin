# RotoWagon — Tenant Admin Console

Dedicated enterprise management console for **RotoWagon Equipment Pvt. Ltd.**

## Capabilities
- **Machinery Inventory & 5-Step Product Wizard**: Add, edit, catalog, and configure specifications for industrial bakery machines.
- **Leads & Quotations CRM**: Filter leads, update pipeline stages, assign sales reps, and export CSV callsets.
- **Trade Fair & Event Management**: Booth campaign configuration and kiosk mode lead capture.
- **Vector QR Code Studio**: Generate scannable QR codes for marketing materials and standees.
- **Enterprise Card & Digital Passes**: Manage Digital ID, Visitor Pass, Parking QR, and Emergency SOS.
- **Industrial WEHIRRE Corporate Design**: Collapsible sidebar navigation rail with left-anchored badges and frozen sticky topbar.
